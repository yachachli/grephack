YIELD PREDICTION

Knowing that fruit will be ready is only half the plan. The team also needs to know how much fruit is likely to come off each block.

VineFlow’s yield capability combines crop counts, block acreage, field boundaries, and observed harvest results to estimate expected tons by block. The field boundary is important because it connects a physical part of the farm to the crop estimate, map, and harvest history.

Why it works: the model can start from the team’s existing physical cluster counts, then improve as actual pick results come in. Rather than treating every block as an average, it has a specific location, acreage, and history.

Why it matters: expected tons turn directly into expected loads, trucks, winery appointments, and machine time. That is the final link between a forecast about fruit and a plan the operator can execute.
